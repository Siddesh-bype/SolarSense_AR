## **Section 2: PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana — Complete** **Guide**

**Official Website:** https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in | **Ministry:** Ministry of New and Renewable Energy
(MNRE)

**Helpline:** 1800-180-3333 (Toll Free) | **Launched:** February 15, 2024 by PM Narendra Modi


PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana is the flagship rooftop solar scheme of the Government of India with a
total outlay of ₹75,021 crore. The scheme targets 1 crore households by 2027 to receive free electricity
up to 300 units per month by installing grid-connected rooftop solar systems.

#### **Subsidy Structure (Central Financial Assistance)**
























|System Capacity|Benchmark<br>Cost|Subsidy %|Subsidy<br>Amount|Your Net<br>Cost*|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1 kW System|₹65,000 –<br>₹80,000|~40–46%|₹30,000|₹35,000 –<br>₹50,000|
|2 kW System|₹1,20,000 –<br>₹1,50,000|~40%|₹60,000|₹60,000 –<br>₹90,000|
|3 kW System|₹1,80,000 –<br>₹2,10,000|~37–43%|₹78,000 (Max)|₹1,02,000 –<br>₹1,32,000|
|Above 3 kW|₹2,10,000+|Capped at 3kW|₹78,000 (Max)|Balance by<br>owner|



*Net cost is indicative. Actual subsidy is based on MNRE benchmark rates which are updated
periodically. Always verify current rates at pmsuryaghar.gov.in before advising users.

#### **How Subsidy is Calculated**


The subsidy structure uses a slab-based formula:

1. For systems up to 2 kW: Central Financial Assistance = 60% of benchmark cost per kW
2. For the 3rd kW (2–3 kW): Central Financial Assistance = 40% of benchmark cost for that kW
3. Total maximum central subsidy = ₹78,000 (for 3 kW and above)
4. Systems above 3 kW: Subsidy capped at ₹78,000; owner pays the difference

#### **Eligibility Criteria**


   - Must be an Indian citizen (individual or joint homeowner)

   - Must own a residential property with valid title/proof

   - Must have a valid electricity consumer connection from a DISCOM

   - Rooftop must have sufficient shadow-free area for panel installation

   - Household must not have previously availed any government solar subsidy

   - Both individual homes and group housing societies (apartments) are eligible


   - No income ceiling — all income groups can apply

#### **Step-by-Step Application Process**


5. Step 1: Visit the official portal — https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in
6. Step 2: Click on 'Apply for Rooftop Solar' on the homepage
7. Step 3: Register using your Aadhaar-linked mobile number — an OTP will be sent for

verification
8. Step 4: Enter your electricity consumer number from your latest bill (selects your DISCOM

automatically)
9. Step 5: Fill in your personal details: name, address, property documents, bank details
10. Step 6: Select your desired system capacity (1 kW, 2 kW, or 3 kW) — portal shows the exact

subsidy you will receive
11. Step 7: Choose from MNRE-empanelled solar vendors listed on the portal for your area
12. Step 8: Selected vendor conducts a free site visit, verifies rooftop suitability, and gives final

system design
13. Step 9: DISCOM issues a feasibility approval and sanctions net metering connection
14. Step 10: Installation takes place (typically 2–3 days for residential systems)
15. Step 11: DISCOM inspects the installation and issues a commissioning certificate
16. Step 12: Upload your bank account details + cancelled cheque on the portal
17. Step 13: Subsidy is credited directly to your bank account within 30 days of commissioning

#### **Important Rules & Restrictions**






- Only ALMM-listed domestic panels qualify — Chinese or non-listed panels disqualify the
application

- GitHub Timeline for installations: Subsidy is valid only after DISCOM commissioning certificate
is issued

- Net metering mandatory: System must be grid-connected; off-grid systems do not qualify

- Only one subsidy per household: Prior government solar subsidies disqualify the applicant

- System size cap: Subsidy capped at 3 kW; larger systems allowed but subsidy doesn't increase

- Bank account must match applicant name: Joint accounts accepted if applicant is listed
